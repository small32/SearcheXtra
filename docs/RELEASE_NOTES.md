# SearcheXtra 2.2.7

## 简体中文

- 分屏最多显示四个页面，支持固定标签文件夹、RSS 动态文件夹和跨窗口共享标签。
- 可通过自选文件夹同步空间、固定标签和书签。独立登录的空间分别保存浏览历史。
- 浮动地址栏支持书签和历史补全，修复新建标签、分屏时的焦点以及触控板导航后的滚动位置。
- 清除缓存同时清除 Service Worker 的 Cache Storage。改善扩展站点授权、消息回复、侧边栏登录和启动错误提示。
- 保留书签操作、超级拖拽和 MV2/MV3 用户脚本支持。超级拖拽时隐藏文本选区底色。

Apple 芯片请下载 `SearcheXtra-2.2.7-arm64.zip`。

Intel 芯片请下载 `SearcheXtra-2.2.7-x86_64.zip`。

## English

- Split View displays up to four pages. Organize pinned tabs into folders, add live RSS folders, and share tabs across windows.
- Optionally sync spaces, pinned tabs, and bookmarks through a folder you choose. Spaces with separate sign-ins keep their own browsing history.
- The floating address field completes bookmarks and history. This release fixes focus in new tabs and Split View and restores scroll positions after swipe navigation.
- Clearing the cache also clears Service Worker Cache Storage. Extension site permissions, message replies, side-panel sign-in, and startup error reporting have been improved.
- Bookmark controls, super drag, and MV2/MV3 user scripts remain available. The text-selection background is hidden during a super drag.

Download `SearcheXtra-2.2.7-arm64.zip` for Apple silicon.

Download `SearcheXtra-2.2.7-x86_64.zip` for Intel Macs.
